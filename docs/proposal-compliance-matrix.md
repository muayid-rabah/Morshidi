## Student-intelligence finding

The repository has a strong **academic-structure intelligence** layer: exact course identity, attempts, progress, eligibility, requirement gaps, direct/joint unlocks, semester options, and bounded degree paths. It does **not** yet have the proposal’s **student-performance intelligence** layer. There is no validated strengths engine, weaknesses engine, risk model, readiness model, or continuous performance model. Free-form LLM inference is not acceptable evidence for any of these gaps.

## Pilot and metric measurement requirements

Before pilot execution, add privacy-reviewed event and survey contracts for: participant/session pseudonymous ID, consent/version, task start/end, baseline plan, Morshidi-assisted plan, viewed recommendations, accepted/rejected suggestions, usefulness/clarity/trust responses, and advisor-reviewed correctness. Do not store raw secrets or unnecessary conversation data. The real pilot must use at least 30 consented Zarqa students, measure the 80% usefulness target with a declared denominator, and compare planning time before/after on the same participants. PROP-086–088 require baselines and must not be marked implemented merely because enabling code exists.

## External-dependency classification

- Zarqa academic authority: resolve six ambiguous prerequisite expressions and validate model definitions.
- University records/data office: authoritative grades, attempts, GPA policy, course outcomes, offerings, cohorts, and data-sharing approval.
- Ethics/privacy stakeholders: consent, retention, minimization, pilot protocol, and approved reporting.
- Students and advisors: ≥30 real participants, ground-truth review, surveys, and baseline/post measurements.
- University IAM/security: SIS contract/sandbox, secure API, SSO metadata/claims, role mapping, and security approval.
- Additional institutions: official curricula/policies and validation for scalability claims.
- LLM provider: server-side credentials and service access; academic decisions must continue without provider authority.

## Ambiguous proposal wording

1. “Cut planning time” states no numeric reduction target. Acceptance must report the measured paired effect without inventing a threshold.
2. “Personal readiness,” “academic risk,” “strengths,” and “weaknesses” are named but not operationally defined. Academic/product owners must approve constructs, labels, inputs, and acceptable error before implementation.
3. “High priority” has no threshold semantics. It cannot be inferred merely from rank without an approved mapping.
4. “Future consequences” may mean prerequisite unlock/delay, graduation timing, or course-outcome risk. The roadmap initially requires an auditable structural delay counterfactual and prohibits graduation-date or offering claims without data.
5. “Every student, anytime” and regional scale do not state availability, concurrency, accessibility, or tenancy targets. These must be made measurable before final scale acceptance.
