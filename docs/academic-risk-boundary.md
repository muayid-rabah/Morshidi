# Academic Risk Boundary

## Deterministic structural signals

Current supported signals are repeated failure in a required course, repeated withdrawal in a required course, a verified required-course bottleneck, and a concentrated verified dependency blockage. They report observed history and structural consequence only. They do not predict failure, assign probability, diagnose cause, or recommend sanctions.

## Predictive academic risk

Predictive risk is `BLOCKED_BY_EXTERNAL_DATA`. It requires governed de-identified cohort records, approved labels/outcomes, temporal holdouts, leakage controls, calibration threshold, subgroup/fairness analysis, institutional/privacy approval, monitoring/drift policy, versioning, retirement process, and human-review policy. Before those conditions, forbidden outputs include risk score, probability, likelihood, predicted grade, “will fail”, or model-generated risk prose.

Raw grades require approved grading-policy semantics before interpretation. Course domains require EVID-017 taxonomy. Cohort modeling requires EVID-018. Internal evidence stays owner-scoped and minimized; institutional use requires approved aggregate governance.
