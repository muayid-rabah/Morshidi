# Morshidi Academic Catalog Database Schema

## Scope

This document describes the Phase 4.1 PostgreSQL schema draft in
`supabase/migrations/0001_academic_catalog.sql`. It implements the approved
Academic Data Model v2.0 as a catalog only. It creates no records and must not
be applied to a Supabase project until review is complete.

Student accounts, profiles, student study plans, course records, grades,
semesters, schedules, recommendations, AI conversations, and authentication
are deliberately excluded. They require their own access and lifecycle design.

## Type strategy

The schema uses `uuid` primary keys generated by `gen_random_uuid()` from
`pgcrypto`, `timestamptz` audit fields, and `numeric(6,2)` for credit hours.
Check constraints are used instead of PostgreSQL ENUM types for controlled
status values. These lists will likely evolve with academic-source review; CHECK
constraints keep each allowed value beside its column and avoid the difficult
removal and lifecycle of shared enum types in future Supabase migrations.

All tables have `created_at` and `updated_at`. The reusable
`public.set_updated_at()` trigger function updates the latter before every row
update.

## Tables and relationships

| Table | Purpose | Key relationships |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `universities` | Supported university identity and metadata. | Parent of faculties, sources, and courses. |
| `faculties` | Faculty within a university. | Belongs to `universities`; parent of majors. |
| `majors` | Academic program within a faculty. | Belongs to `faculties`; parent of study plans. |
| `academic_sources` | Traceable official/manual academic evidence, including snapshots and hashes. | Belongs to `universities`; referenced by plans, plan courses, and equivalencies. |
| `study_plans` | Versioned curriculum for a major. | Belongs to `majors`; optionally traces to `academic_sources`. |
| `requirement_groups` | Machine-readable requirement bucket in a plan. | Belongs to `study_plans`; parent of plan-course placements. |
| `courses` | University-wide course identity; has no credit-hour column. | Belongs to `universities`; can be referenced-only. |
| `study_plan_courses` | A course's plan-specific placement and published academic properties. | Belongs to a plan, course, requirement group, and optional source. |
| `course_dependency_groups` | AND-combined groups for prerequisites or corequisites. | Belongs to a study-plan course. |
| `course_dependency_options` | OR options inside one dependency group. | Belongs to a dependency group and references a course. |
| `course_equivalencies` | Source-to-target official equivalency, replacement, or legacy-code relation. | Both courses are constrained to its university. |

## Integrity and deletion behavior

The hierarchy uses `ON DELETE RESTRICT`: catalog parents cannot be removed
while referenced records exist. This protects published academic evidence and
requires an explicit, reviewed deletion sequence. The only cascades are from a
study-plan course to its dependency groups and from a dependency group to its
options, because those rows have no meaning independently of their parent.

Important declarative safeguards:

- `study_plans` is identified by `(major_id, plan_number, effective_year)`;
  its unique expression index treats a missing effective year as one distinct
  value, and a plan number is not globally unique.
- `requirement_groups` is unique by `(study_plan_id, group_code)`, validates
  hours and min/max course boundaries, and exposes `(id, study_plan_id)` as a
  composite candidate key.
- `study_plan_courses` is unique by `(study_plan_id, course_id)`. Its composite
  foreign key `(requirement_group_id, study_plan_id)` references the matching
  requirement-group candidate key, so a group cannot be attached to another
  plan's course. `validate_study_plan_course_university()` also rejects a
  course or optional source from another university on insert or relevant
  update.
- `courses` is unique by `(university_id, course_code)`. `name_ar` and
  `name_en` may both be null for a `referenced_only` course. A `known` course
  must have at least one display name.
- dependency groups are unique by parent, dependency type, and group number;
  options are unique by group and dependency course.
- `public.prevent_self_course_dependency()` rejects self-dependencies when an
  option is written, a dependency group is moved, or a plan course's course
  identity is changed.
- equivalencies reject identical source/target courses and invalid date ranges.
  Composite foreign keys to `(courses.id, courses.university_id)` ensure both
  referenced courses belong to the equivalency's university. A null-safe unique
  index prevents duplicate relationships with the same date boundaries, and
  `validate_course_equivalency_source_university()` validates optional evidence.

`validate_study_plan_source_university()` ensures a plan's optional source is
owned by its university. `validate_dependency_course_university()` permits
referenced-only dependency courses but rejects dependency courses from another
university, including when a dependency group is moved. Academic-source
ownership cannot be changed while the source is referenced, preventing a
previously valid relationship from becoming cross-university. Ownership moves
of a populated faculty, major, study plan, or referenced course are also
rejected, rather than allowing an existing valid descendant relationship to
become cross-university without being revalidated.

The migration keeps `raw_prerequisite_text` as text on `study_plan_courses`.
It is independent from structured dependency rows and must never be overwritten
by a later logical interpretation.

## Indexes

Unique constraints supply indexes for core identities and dependency traversal.
Additional indexes support faculty/major joins, source lookups by university and
status, plan lookup by major and source, direct course-code lookup, plan-course
source and requirement-group filtering, dependency-course traversal, and both
sides of an equivalency lookup including its source. These are targeted catalog
access paths rather than a broad set of speculative indexes.

## Unresolved academic references

No equivalency rows are inserted. In particular, `0300103` ↔ `0300104` and
`0301241` ↔ `0301245` remain unresolved academic questions. Referenced-only
courses such as `0300103` and `0301241` can be represented with null names and
`catalog_status = 'referenced_only'` without inventing an equivalency.

## RLS and authentication

RLS is enabled on all 11 public catalog tables as defense in depth. No end-user
RLS policies exist yet, and `anon` and `authenticated` are explicitly revoked
from every catalog table. This migration therefore does not expose catalog data
through the end-user Data API by default. Deliberate API grants and policies
will be designed later; backend/database-owner access is separate from
end-user Data API access.

## Student academic state (Phase 6.2)

`student_academic_profiles` is a private, one-per-authenticated-user profile.
It owns a restrictive `study_plan_id`, optional non-negative cumulative GPA and
earned-credit facts, and a positive GPA scale whenever GPA is supplied. The
profile and scale must be supplied together. A profile may not move to another
study plan after it has attempts.

`student_course_attempts` stores repeatable, raw reported outcomes for a
profile and canonical course. Outcomes are limited to `PASSED`, `FAILED`,
`IN_PROGRESS`, and `WITHDRAWN`; optional numbered attempts are unique per
profile/course only when a sequence is supplied. Optional source, term, date,
and raw grade fields preserve reporting facts without interpreting grades.

The attempt trigger derives the profile plan's university through the catalog
hierarchy and requires it to equal the attempted course's university. This
permits same-university `referenced_only` courses but prevents cross-university
attempts without storing a redundant university identifier. Profile deletion
cascades to attempts; catalog plans and courses remain restrictive.

RLS is enabled on both student tables. `authenticated` users receive only
owner-scoped CRUD policies (`auth.uid()` for profiles and the owning profile
for attempts); `anon` receives no grant or policy. Both tables use the shared
`set_updated_at` trigger.
