## Status precedence

For course/intent validation: `INVALID` precedes `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, which precedes `VALID`. For demand: `SUPPRESSED` applies before metric exposure; otherwise `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`, `PARTIAL`, or `AVAILABLE` follows the demand policy. Missing optional offerings/capacity adds exact reason/quality flags without turning valid intent counts into zero or invalidating intent.
